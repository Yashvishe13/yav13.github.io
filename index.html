About

Hi, I'm Yash Vishe, a Master's student in Data Science at UC San Diego, with a passion for advancing artificial intelligence and machine learning. I hold a Bachelor's degree in Computer Engineering from the University of Mumbai, India.

My research focuses on Large Language Models, Multi-modal AI, and Music Information Retrieval. I'm currently working as a Software Engineering Intern at San Diego Supercomputer Center, where I build LLM-powered search systems and develop ETL pipelines for large-scale ML workflows.

Previously, I worked as a Research Assistant at the McAuley Lab, UC San Diego, where I focused on AI-driven code generation and music information retrieval. Prior to that, I was a Software Engineer at Media.net in India, developing scalable ETL pipelines and optimizing data workflows for high-performance ad-tech systems.

I'm particularly interested in developing AI systems that can understand and generate music, as well as building scalable machine learning solutions.
