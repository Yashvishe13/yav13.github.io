Yash Vishe

AI Engineer & Researcher at Vyasa AI

I am an AI Engineer and Researcher at Vyasa AI, where I work on behaviour-based continual improvement of agents that unifies context across enterprise softwares through self-correction loops. I received my Masters in Data Science from UC San Diego, where I worked with Prof. Julian McAuley and Prof. Amarnath Gupta, and my Bachelors in Computer Engineering from the University of Mumbai.

My research focuses on continual evolution, multimodal reasoning and reinforcement learning. I am currently interested in learning through infinite generative environments. My other research interest is also in Musical Intelligence and audio preservation across generative edits.
